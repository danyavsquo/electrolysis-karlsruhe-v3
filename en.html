Why electrolysis

  • The only permanent method

    In electrolysis every hair is treated individually at the root. The follicle is disabled and grows no new hair.

  • Every hair colour

    Blonde, grey, red and very fine hair included — precisely where light-based methods fail, because there is no pigment for them to work on.

  • Every skin type

    The treatment works on the individual hair, not on the contrast with your skin — independent of skin tone, and possible on tanned skin.

Any questions?

  1. 1

    Step 1: Consultation

    We look at the area, go through your medical history and any contraindications, and discuss what is realistically possible.

  2. 2

    Step 2: Trial treatment

    A small area to get acquainted. You feel what it is like, and we see how your skin responds.

  3. 3

    Step 3: Treatment

    Hair by hair, with a sterile single-use needle. Session length depends on the area and hair density.

  4. 4

    Step 4: Aftercare

    You go home with a care recommendation. Slight redness is normal and usually settles quickly.
